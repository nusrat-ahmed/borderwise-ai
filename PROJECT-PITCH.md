# BorderWise AI — Project Pitch

## One-Sentence Summary

BorderWise AI is a prototype for assisting cross-border financial workflows through transaction intake, transparent rule-based risk screening, and a reviewable demo history.

## Problem

Cross-border financial workflows may involve complex transaction details and compliance checks. A reviewer needs a clear way to inspect transaction information and understand why a transaction may need additional review.

## Proposed Approach

1. Accept transaction details through a web interface.
2. Validate submitted fields through a backend API.
3. Apply illustrative, rule-based risk indicators.
4. Present the result and available transaction history for review.

## What the Prototype Demonstrates

- A browser-based transaction workflow.
- Backend validation and API endpoints.
- Explainable rule-based risk screening.
- Local demo records and automated tests.

## What It Does Not Claim

- It is not connected to a real bank or payment network.
- It does not transfer money.
- It does not provide legal or regulatory approval.
- Its risk logic is not a trained or validated machine-learning model.
- Its local database is not a tamper-proof audit system.

## Intended Audience

Competition judges, developers, and researchers evaluating an early-stage concept. The demo should use synthetic data only.

## Future Work

Potential next steps include a documented synthetic evaluation dataset, expanded tests, stronger security controls, and carefully authorized integrations with reliable external data providers. Real deployment would require substantial engineering, security, privacy, and regulatory review.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the local web application.
2. Submit a synthetic demo transaction.
3. Review validation and risk-screening output.
4. Inspect the demo history.
5. Explain the rule-based approach and its limitations honestly.
